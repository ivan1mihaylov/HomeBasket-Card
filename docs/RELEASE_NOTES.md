**A known barcode no longer interrupts.** Scanning something HomeBasket already
knows puts it on the list and in the recent strip and gets out of the way, so
one product after another can be scanned without stopping. `open_known` brings
back the old behaviour of opening the product for editing.

**A shopping list that counts says so.** With
[HomeBasket Lists](https://github.com/ivan1mihaylov/HomeBasket-Lists) as the
shopping list, scanning something already on it says *milk on the list: 2 now*
instead of shrugging with "already on the list" — or says it was kept, when the
list is set to keep what it has.

**The camera sheet behaves.** It no longer jumps as the stream arrives, and no
longer flashes the browser's own white ground or grey play button: the frame is
black and a fixed size from the start, and the picture fades in. Two sheets can
no longer stack, which is what used to look like a scan that never finished.
Arriving from a home screen shortcut, the sheet says the camera needs one tap
rather than reporting a refusal — and the whole black square is that tap.

The photo now sits at the top of the product sheet, in the middle of it, and the
README spells the home screen shortcut out field by field.
