**A barcode can be typed in by hand.**

A product's sheet now lists its barcodes, however many it has, with a field to
add one. That is what a product with no barcode needs — one a shopping list
configured, or anything else never scanned: give it a barcode and scanning
finds the name, picture and shop already set, rather than making a second
product.

Such a product shows as **No barcode** until it has one.

Needs HomeBasket 0.11.0.

**The dropdowns follow the theme.** The list a select drops down is drawn by
the browser, and was coming up white on a dark dashboard. It now follows the
colour scheme the theme is in.
